The device is equipped with two sensors, the Sensirion SCD41 that measures carbon dioxide, relative humidity and temperature. The remaining sensor is the Bosch BME690 that measures gas, air pressure, temperature and humidity.

SCD41 

    Is a photoacoustic sensor, the sensor emits infrared light that are absorbed by Co2 molecules. They absorb specific wavelengths of IR light.
